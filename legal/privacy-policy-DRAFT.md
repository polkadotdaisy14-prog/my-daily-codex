## 1. What we collect

We collect only what the service needs to function:

**Account information.** Your email address and whatever profile details you provide when you sign up (including via Google sign-in, which shares your name and email with us).

**Birth data.** The date, time, and place of birth you enter — plus your name if you choose to provide it (used for numerology). This is the core input your readings are computed from. Birth data is personal information: we treat it accordingly.

**Your content.** Your daily readings history, Pattern Log entries, observation notes, and any other content you create in the app.

**Payment information.** Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We **never receive, see, or store your card numbers** — Stripe handles all of that directly. We do receive records of your subscription status (active, cancelled, billing dates) so we can gate paid features.

**Technical information.** Basic device and browser information, login timestamps, and error logs needed to keep the service running and secure. We also use local storage on your device (see §7).

We do **not** collect precise geolocation beyond the birthplace you enter, we run **no advertising**, and we use **no third-party ad trackers**.

## 2. How we use your information

- To create your account and keep you signed in.
- To compute your natal charts and daily readings from the birth data you provide.
- To store your Pattern Log and reading history so you can revisit them.
- To manage your subscription (via Stripe) and provide customer support.
- To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.
